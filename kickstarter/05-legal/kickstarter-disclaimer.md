# Kickstarter legal / positioning disclaimer

## What Kickstarter is

Kickstarter is a **rewards-based crowdfunding** platform. Backers pledge money to help create a project and typically receive **rewards** (early access, credits, merch, etc.).

Kickstarter pledges are **not**:
- equity or shares in CrypterChat / ChatScan
- securities or investment contracts (in the usual Kickstarter model)
- a promise of profit, tokens with guaranteed value, or ROI

## What you should say on the campaign page

Use language like:
- “Back this project”
- “Pledge”
- “Help us build”
- “Rewards”

Avoid language like:
- “Invest and get equity”
- “Guaranteed returns”
- “Buy tokens that will pump”

If you later want equity investors, use a **separate, regulated** channel (angel round, equity crowdfunding where legal). Do not mix that into Kickstarter reward copy.

## Privacy claim accuracy

Only claim what the product does today:
- ChatScan stores and displays **HASH/ID** commitments
- Message **contents are not viewable** on ChatScan
- X11 Blockchain is **under development** — be explicit about testnet vs mainnet timelines
