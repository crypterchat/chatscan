# How much money can ChatScan raise on Kickstarter?

This is an **estimate**, not a guarantee. Kickstarter outcomes depend on audience, prelaunch email list, press, and video quality.

## Hard market facts (Technology / Apps)

| Signal | What the data says |
|--------|--------------------|
| Overall Kickstarter success | ~42% of projects fund |
| Technology category success | ~33% |
| **Apps subcategory success** | **~9%** (worst tech bucket) |
| Tech goals under $10K | ~45% success |
| Tech goals $10K–$30K | ~30% success |
| Tech goals $30K–$100K | ~20% success |
| Tech goals $100K+ | ~9% success |
| Apps median goal | ~$17,300 |

Sources: Kickstarter platform stats (Jan 2025 overview), BackerBench Technology / Apps benchmarks.

## Realistic funding scenarios for ChatScan

| Scenario | Primary goal | Expected raise if funded | Likelihood* |
|----------|--------------|--------------------------|-------------|
| **Conservative (recommended)** | **$15,000** | $15,000–$28,000 | Medium if you have 500+ warm emails |
| Base | $25,000 | $25,000–$45,000 | Lower without strong audience |
| Optimistic | $50,000 | $50,000–$90,000 | Needs viral privacy community + press |
| Unlikely / stretch ceiling | $100,000+ | Variable | Rare for app-only campaigns |

\*Likelihood assumes a 30–35 day campaign, solid video, and active outreach. Without a prelaunch list, treat odds as **low**.

## Recommended strategy

1. **Set primary goal to $15,000** (minimum to ship X11 testnet adapter + hardened explorer).
2. Publish **stretch goals** at $25K / $40K / $75K (security review, mobile app bridge, localization).
3. Build **1,000+ prelaunch followers/emails** before launch — this is the biggest controllable lever.
4. Position as **open-source privacy infrastructure** (Technology → Apps), not “crypto get rich.”
5. Remember: Kickstarter funds are **pledges for rewards**, not investor equity.

## Net cash after fees (rule of thumb)

Kickstarter + payment processing typically take ~8–10% combined.

| Gross raised | Approx. net after fees |
|--------------|------------------------|
| $15,000 | ~$13,500–$13,800 |
| $25,000 | ~$22,500–$23,000 |
| $50,000 | ~$45,000–$46,000 |

## Bottom line

- **Safe ask:** **$15,000**
- **If campaign lands well:** **$20,000–$40,000** is a credible band
- **$100,000+** is possible only with a large existing CrypterChat community and media lift — do not plan the business on that number alone
